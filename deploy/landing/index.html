Mii-nagement isn't from the App Store yet, so the first time you open it macOS asks you to confirm it.

  1. 1
    Open Mii-nagement.dmg and drag Mii-nagement into Applications.
  2. 2
    Open it. When macOS says it can't check it, click Done.
  3. 3
    Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  4. 4
    Click Open Anyway again and enter your password. From then on it opens like any app.

Prefer Terminal? xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Mii-nagement.app

Your download has started!

Mii-nagement isn't signed by Microsoft yet, so the first time Windows asks you to confirm it.

  1. 1
    Open Mii-nagement-Setup.exe.
  2. 2
    If Windows says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. 3
    Click through the installer (no admin password needed) and start Mii-nagement from the Start menu or your desktop.

Everything it needs comes with it: its own Python, Git and Node. Your residents think with the AI you already use: log in to Claude Code, Codex or Gemini once, or add an OpenRouter key in Settings.
